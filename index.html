LiDAR-Based Landslide Susceptibility Assessment

This project visualizes landslide susceptibility across Mercer Island using LiDAR-derived DEMs, slope analysis, hillshade modeling, and terrain classification techniques.

Multiple terrain models revealed elevated landslide risk near the I-90 highway corridor and the outer slopes of Mercer Island.

The DEM model reclassified elevation values above 500 as potentially susceptible landslide areas based on prior LiDAR hazard assessment research.

Hillshade and slope models revealed depressions, cracks, terrain irregularities, and steep slope gradients associated with potential landslide activity.

Together, the models demonstrate how LiDAR terrain visualization can support hazard mitigation planning and geospatial risk assessment.
